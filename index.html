This Park Has a Story.
Are You Listening?

Montefiore Park is alive with murals, color, and community history — but its design treats it as a corridor, not a canvas. We're proposing targeted arts-and-culture interventions to turn a pass-through into a place people actually stay.

  • Explore the Vision

The Murals Are Invisible

World-class community murals line the park walls, yet every bench faces the path — not the art. Pedestrians pass within metres of these works without ever registering them as something to stop and see.

  • See the proposals

No Reason to Linger

Without seating alcoves, lighting, or interpretive signage oriented toward the artwork, visitors have no physical or psychological cue to slow down. The park's spatial layout actively funnels movement rather than inviting pause.

  • Our seating strategy

Culture Without an Audience

Montefiore holds rotating murals, cultural history, and community-commissioned art — all without scheduled programming, events, or activation. Culture without an audience fades. We're proposing a recurring arts calendar to fix that.

Our Arts & Culture Proposals

Six targeted interventions — from seating design to live programming — that work together to transform Montefiore Park from a corridor into a cultural destination where people choose to stay.

Mural-Facing Seating Alcoves

Curved bench clusters installed directly opposite each mural panel, angled so the natural resting posture faces the art. Planters and low hedges define each alcove as a self-contained 'room,' creating spatial variety in what is currently one long undifferentiated corridor.

Warm Uplighting + Interpretive Panels

Warm-tone uplighting on each mural extends the park's cultural life into the evening, making the art readable and compelling after dark. Weatherproof interpretive panels beside each work share the artist's name, intent, and community story — giving visitors a reason to stop and read.

Live Mural-Making Sessions

Monthly public mural-making workshops led by the original artists or local youth — turning passive viewers into active participants. Sessions are scheduled on weekends and advertised through local schools and the community board.

Open-Air Performances

A small performance platform sited at the widest point of the park, used for open mics, poetry slams, and acoustic sets. Performers face the murals — so the audience naturally sits with the artwork in their field of vision.

Mural Cinema Nights

Monthly outdoor film screenings where the selected film connects thematically to one of the park's murals. A short pre-screening talk by the mural's artist bridges film and visual art, building a new audience for both.

Weekend Arts Market

A Saturday rotating market featuring Bronx-based artists, printmakers, and craftspeople — positioned so every stall is visible from the mural wall. The market creates a reason to visit the park as a destination, not a shortcut.
